# Puppet House — 3 minute demo script

## Recording setup

- Record at 1440×900 or 1920×1080, 30 fps, browser chrome hidden where practical.
- Use one host browser, one guest browser and one spectator browser. Keep each in a separate profile/incognito context.
- Use a fresh party with short, screen-readable contestant names and secrets.
- Enable Nexconn and optional AI only after live credential QA. Never show environment values or the App Secret.
- Prepare cuts so viewers do not watch timers or loading screens.

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook

**Visual:** Landing page, then a fast cut to three contestants arguing in the house.

**Narration:** “What if a reality-show contestant had its own agenda—and you could only control it three times? This is Puppet House: AI contestants, human puppeteers, hidden agendas.”

## 0:15–0:35 — Create the social experiment

**Visual:** Create a contestant. Highlight personality, public persona, private secret and secret mission. Create a party and copy the invite.

**Narration:** “Every producer authors one autonomous contestant. Its personality is public; its secret and mission are not. Up to four friends can join a private party, and empty seats are filled by the house.”

## 0:35–1:05 — Autonomous chaos and scarce control

**Visual:** Split host and guest views. Let public messages arrive. Open Producer Cam and send one whisper. Show three whisper pips become two.

**Narration:** “The contestants talk, form bonds and turn on each other by themselves. A producer gets exactly three private whispers for the whole season. The contestant interprets the advice—it does not become a puppet on a string.”

## 1:05–1:30 — Nexconn and privacy

**Visual:** NEXCONN LIVE indicator. Send a house event and show it arriving in the second browser. Show different private missions in the two producer views.

**Narration:** “Nexconn carries live group messages and private producer whispers. The game server remains authoritative: each browser receives only its own mission, whispers, private DMs and ballot.”

## 1:30–1:50 — Audience agency

**Visual:** Spectator room, named audience chat, trigger Memory Leak or Double Agent. Cut back to the house reaction.

**Narration:** “The audience is not a view counter. Spectators chat and trigger bounded twists. During each ballot, House Pulse lets them predict the outcome, watch live consensus build and earn Oracle points for accurate social reads.”

## 1:50–2:20 — Vote and consequence

**Visual:** Both producers privately select ballots. Show ballot count without choices. Host reveals only when all eligible ballots are ready. Show elimination and changed trust/popularity.

**Narration:** “Ballots are independent and hidden until the reveal. Immunity and voting eligibility are enforced by the server. Elimination changes who can coach, vote and win.”

## 2:20–2:45 — Finale and receipts

**Visual:** Final ballot, winner, score breakdown, Season Storylines and finale-card download. Click Run It Back and show both producers entering the same fresh lobby.

**Narration:** “Every season ends with a winner, automatic storylines and a shareable finale card. And when the group wants revenge, Run It Back preserves the party and cast with fresh whisper budgets—no re-inviting, no recasting.”

## 2:45–3:00 — Why it can grow

**Visual:** Landing page and party lobby; optional montage of mobile and spectator views.

**Narration:** “Puppet House is a repeatable social format for friend groups, campus events and streamer communities: themed seasons, character packs, private tournaments and branded interactive shows. Bring three friends. Lose them strategically.”
